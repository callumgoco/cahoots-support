Support

We’re here for account, invite, and check-in questions.

Contact

Email callumgoconnor@gmail.com and include your display name plus a short description of what went wrong.

Joining a group

Ask a member for their invite link or six-character code. Open the link on your iPhone with Cahoots installed, or paste the code in the app.

Sign in

Use Sign in with Apple or email. If you use email confirmation, confirm the message before the first sign-in.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile → delete account. That removes your profile and owned data from our servers.
