How it works

One shared goal. Your people only.

No public feed. No strangers. Just a small crew agreeing on a daily check-in and keeping each other honest.

Invite your crew

Create a private group and share a six-character code. Membership stays invite-only.

Agree on the check-in

Vote on one clear daily target — reps, minutes, or a simple done/not-done.

Show up together

Log your day, optionally add a short proof clip, and unlock the crew feed when you’ve posted.

Built for consistency

Honour system, with receipts

You enter the number. Short clips stay private to the group and expire. Spoilers stay locked until you’ve checked in yourself.
