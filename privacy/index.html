Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

Cahoots is a private group fitness check-in app. This page explains what we collect and why.

Account

When you sign in with Apple or email, we store the account identifiers needed to keep you signed in (such as your user ID, display name, and email if you provide one). Sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

Group activity

We store group membership, challenges, votes, check-in amounts, scores, and short workout proof clips you choose to upload. Clips are private to your group and are removed when they expire or when you delete your account.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device push token so we can send reminders and friend activity alerts you turn on. Lock-screen text does not include workout quantities.

Diagnostics

We may collect crash and performance diagnostics through Apple system frameworks to keep the app reliable. We do not sell your data.

Deletion

You can delete your account in the app. That removes your profile and owned data from our servers, including related clips where possible.
